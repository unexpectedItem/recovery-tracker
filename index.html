What the measures tell us

Peak fear tracks intensity. Recovery time tracks how quickly your nervous system settles. Sleep affects emotional regulation. Avoidance shows whether grief is shrinking your life. Triggers reveal patterns. Grounding response shows whether the techniques are reducing physiological escalation.
